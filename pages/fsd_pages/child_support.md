## How to Pay Your Child Support

There are multiple options to make a child support payment. Be sure you have your 8 digit MACSS case number.

1. :green[**By Phone:**] Call 1-888-761-3665 and pay by credit or debit card.
2. :green[**Cash Payment:**] PayNearMe makes it easy to make a child support payment at locations nationwide. Participating stores nclude Walmart, Casey's General Store, CVS Pharmacy, 7-Eleven, Family Dollar, etc. PayNearMe cash payments charge $1.99 per transaction. You can register at: [https://PayNearMe.com/MOchildsupport](https://PayNearMe.com/MOchildsupport)
3. :green[**Online:**] You may use Apply Pay, Google Pay, credit cards, and/or PayPal to make monthly child support payments. You can make one-time payments or set up recurring payments. There is a 2.5% convenience fee for each transaction. To get started you must visit the Missouri Family Support Payment Center website and carefully follow the instructions at: [https://mo.smartchildsupport.com](https://mo.smartchildsupport.com)
4. :green[**By mail:**] Send a check or money order to: 

> *Family Support Payment Center*   
> P.O. Box #109002   
> Jefferson City, MO 65110-9002

You must include the MACSS case number to make sure your payment goes to the appropriate case. The Family Support Payment Center will then forward the payments.
